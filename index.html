TRADITIONS WE REVERE

The Sacred Gujarati Vidhis We Immortalize દરેક વિધિ પાછળ રહેલા સાચા અર્થ અને લાગણીઓનું સિનેમેટિક દર્શન

Explore how Anant Productions captures every sacred milestone of an authentic Gujarati wedding with heartfelt dignity and artistic grandeur.

01
ચાંદલો માટલી અને ગોળ ધાણા

Chandlo Matli & Gol Dhana

The sacred red vermilion mark and distribution of coriander seeds with jaggery, sealing lifelong devotion with elders' blessings.

02
મંડપ મુહૂર્ત અને ગણેશ સ્થાપના

Mandva & Ganesh Sthapana

Planting the sacred wooden pillar and invoking Lord Ganesha amidst Vedic chants, conch shells, and women singing traditional mangal geet.

03
ગૃહ શાંતિ અને પીઠી

Grah Shanti & Radiant Pithi

Sun-drenched yellow marigolds, pure organic chandan and haldi paste massaged by sisters and aunts amid laughter, music, and splashing floral water.

04
મોસાળું / મામેરું

Mameru / Mosalu Procession

The grand, emotional entry of the bride's maternal uncles bringing heirlooms, silver coin trays, and tears of adoration for their niece.

05
રાસ-ગરબા અને સનેડો ની રાત

Sanedo & Raas Garba Night

Dandiya sticks clashing in sync, dynamic dodhiya circles, kaleidoscope silhouettes, and the entire family dancing until the early hours.

06
જાન આગમન અને પોંખવું (પોખાણા)

Jaan Agaman & Ponkvu

The groom arrives on horseback or vintage car with dhol band, greeted by mother-in-law with an aarti thali, pinch of salt, and the teasing nose-pull.

07
હસ્તમેળાપ અને મંગળફેરા

Hasta Melap & Mangalphera

The union of palms under the holy varamala, walking four pheras around the agni representing Dharma, Artha, Kama, and Moksha.

08
કંકુ પગલાં અને ભાવુક વિદાય

Kanku Pagla & Vidaai

Auspicious red vermilion footprints marking the daughter of the house stepping forward into her new universe, amidst heartfelt embrace and tears.
