TRADITIONS WE REVERE

The Sacred Gujarati Vidhis We Immortalize દરેક વિધિ પાછળ રહેલા સાચા અર્થ અને લાગણીઓનું સિનેમેટિક દર્શન

We understand the sanctity, exact timing, and emotional depth of authentic Gujarati wedding rituals. We never interrupt the Panditji or ask families to recreate sacred moments.

01

Chandlo Matli & Gol Dhana

ચાંદલો માટલી અને ગોળ ધાણા

The sacred vermilion mark and distribution of coriander seeds with jaggery, sealing lifelong devotion with elders' blessings.

02

Mandva & Ganesh Sthapana

મંડપ મુહૂર્ત અને ગણેશ સ્થાપના

Planting the sacred pillar and invoking Lord Ganesha amidst Vedic chants, conch shells, and women singing traditional mangal geet.

03

Grah Shanti & Radiant Pithi

ગૃહ શાંતિ અને પીઠી

Sun-drenched yellow marigolds, pure chandan and haldi paste massaged by sisters and aunts amid laughter, music, and floral water.

04

Mameru / Mosalu Procession

મોસાળું / મામેરું

The grand, emotional entry of the bride's maternal uncles bringing traditional heirlooms, sweets, and tears of adoration for their niece.

05

Sanedo & Raas Garba Night

રાસ-ગરબા અને સનેડો ની રાત

Dandiya sticks clashing in sync, dynamic dodhiya circles, kaleidoscope silhouettes, and the entire family dancing until the early hours.

06

Jaan Agaman & Ponkvu

જાન આગમન અને પોંખવું (પોખાણા)

The groom arrives with dhol band, greeted by mother-in-law with an aarti thali, pinch of salt, and the teasing playful nose-pull.

07

Hasta Melap & Mangalphera

હસ્તમેળાપ અને મંગળફેરા

The union of palms under the holy varamala, walking four sacred pheras around the agni representing Dharma, Artha, Kama, and Moksha.

08

Kanku Pagla & Vidaai

કંકુ પગલાં અને ભાવુક વિદાય

Auspicious red vermilion footprints marking the daughter stepping forward into her new universe, amidst heartfelt embrace and tears.

Behind every frame is our commitment to cultural respect, technical mastery, and thoughtful service.

Reverence for Elders (Baa-Dada)

In traditional Gujarati families, grandparents and elder relatives carry decades of love and blessings. We make sure Baa and Dada are never rushed or made uncomfortable. We capture their proud glances, hands held in prayer, and tears of joy without intrusive gear.

Respectful Sacred Mandap Presence

A Vedic wedding is a sacred spiritual milestone. We coordinate silently with the Panditji, anticipate the timing of Hasta Melap, Mangal Phera, and Kanku Pagla, and position our lenses so family members in the mandap enjoy uninterrupted sightlines.

Warm Skin Tones & Timeless Color Grading

We reject extreme trendy filters that look dated after a few years. Instead, we preserve rich Patola silks, authentic turmeric haldi yellows, deep red velvet lehengas, and natural Indian skin tones with editorial clarity and analog warmth.

Fast Delivery for Family Groups

We know relatives across Gujarat, Mumbai, and overseas eagerly await photos and videos. We deliver vertical highlight reels optimized for WhatsApp within days, allowing your extended family to relive the memories while celebration spirits are high.
