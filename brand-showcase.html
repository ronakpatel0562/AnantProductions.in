Design Rationale & Brand DNA

1. The Infinity Lemniscate

The name Anant translates directly to infinite, boundless, and eternal. The continuous ribbon immortalizes the couple's lifelong bond and generational memories.

2. The Integrated Iris Diaphragm

We eliminated the literal hand-drawn camera outline (which looked like amateur clip-art). In its place, the right loop seamlessly resolves into a precision 6-blade camera aperture, declaring professional cinematography expertise.

3. Royal Gujarat Color Palette

Crafted with Imperial Obsidian (`#0A0908`), Royal Gujarat Velvet Crimson (`#1F070B`), and Champagne Leaf Gold (`#D4AF37`), conveying heritage luxury without looking artificial.

4. Timeless Typography

Replacing the casual cursive handwriting with high-fashion, classical Roman serif Cinzel paired with geometric Montserrat. This establishes immediate credibility and high booking fee prestige.
