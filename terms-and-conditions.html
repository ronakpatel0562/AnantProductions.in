Terms of Service & Studio Retainer Policies

Transparent, fair, and professional agreements built on mutual trust. We guarantee dedicated senior artist allocation, clear delivery timelines, and zero hidden charges.

Last Updated: October 2026 • Applicable to all wedding services contracted with Anant Productions

1. Agreement Scope & Studio Philosophy

These Terms and Conditions govern all wedding photography, cinematography, color grading, drone aerial filming, and heirloom album production services provided by Anant Productions (headquartered on Sindhu Bhavan Road, Bodakdev, Ahmedabad, Gujarat).

"We don't count photos; we capture your wedding story."

Unlike operators who impose rigid numerical shutter quotas or micro-managed crew counts, Anant Productions deploys an expertly balanced ensemble of senior photographers and cinematographers tailored to your specific guest count, multi-location itinerary, and ritual sacredness.

2. Date Reservation & Retainer Fee

Due to the auspicious nature of Gujarati wedding muhurats (particularly in November–February and May–July), demand for specific dates is exceptionally concentrated across Gujarat.

  • Retainer Requirement: A calendar date is exclusively locked for your family only upon receipt of the agreed booking advance retainer fee and signed quotation confirmation.
  • Exclusivity Promise: Once your date is retained, Anant Productions turns away all other couple inquiries for that calendar slot. Consequently, retainer fees are non-refundable.
  • Payment Milestones: Standard payment schedule is structured transparently: 40% advance retainer upon date booking, 50% on the first event day, and 10% upon final delivery of master galleries and albums.

3. Promised Delivery Milestone Timelines

We recognize that waiting months for wedding memories causes anxiety. We commit to the following structured post-production timeline:

48 Hours Sneak-peek portrait teasers ready for instant WhatsApp & Instagram sharing
3 to 4 Weeks Complete curated RAW image archive & master color-retouched high-res gallery
4 to 6 Weeks Signature 4K cinematic highlight film + full ceremony documentary reels
30 Days Artisanal hand-bound heirloom photo album delivered to your doorstep post-approval

All delivered digital files are transferred via high-speed, SSL-secured cloud vaults accessible worldwide, allowing your NRI relatives in the US, UK, Canada, and Africa to view in original pristine quality.

4. Coverage Scope: One Side vs. Both Sides (+₹8,500 Multi-Location)

To provide absolute pricing fairness without surprise venue charges:

  • One Side Coverage: Focuses comprehensively on either the Groom's or Bride's side of events.
  • Both Sides (Same Venue): If both families host ceremonies at the same venue or resort, our synchronized crew covers both families seamlessly.
  • Both Sides (Different Venues / Simultaneous Events): If the Groom and Bride host morning rituals (e.g. Mandap Muhurat or Haldi) simultaneously at two separate locations (e.g., bride in Naranpura and groom in Bodakdev), a transparent add-on fee of +₹8,500 applies to deploy independent senior camera units, sound kits, and dedicated logistics.

5. Photo Curation & Retouching Standards

We do not deliver raw uncurated rejects (blinks, test shots, out-of-focus misfires). Every delivered frame is hand-curated by our senior editing director:

  • High-Resolution RAW Master Archive: Every genuine moment, emotion, family group photo, and ritual is preserved in full resolution without artificial limits.
  • Master Color Retouching: Signature fine-art grading honoring rich Gujarati silks, authentic skin tones, mandap sacred fires, and floral decor.
  • Raw Video Footage: Uncut 4K ceremony footage and ambient audio masters can be transferred to a client-provided hard drive upon request.

6. Creative Discretion & Event Cooperation

Wedding photography is an interpretive fine art. Anant Productions exercises professional artistic discretion concerning angles, lighting techniques, and cinematic cuts. We work cordially alongside event planners, pandits, and decorators.

The client agrees to designate a family coordinator (brother, sister, cousin) during group stages to assist our lead photographer in identifying key immediate elders (Baa, Dada, Nana, Nani) and VIP guests.

7. Copyright & Personal Client License

As creators, statutory copyright remains with Anant Productions. However, upon receipt of full payment, the Client is granted a worldwide, perpetual, royalty-free personal license to:

  • Print unlimited photos, canvases, wall art, and books for personal and family use.
  • Share photos and films on personal social media platforms (Instagram, Facebook, YouTube).
  • Distribute files freely to family members and guests.

Neither party may sell or license the media to third-party commercial brands or advertisers without explicit reciprocal mutual written agreement.

8. Rescheduling, Date Adjustments & Force Majeure

If an event must be rescheduled due to bona fide family emergencies or natural acts of God, Anant Productions will apply 100% of the received retainer to any new date within 12 months, subject entirely to studio calendar availability on the revised date.

9. Legal Jurisdiction

Any legal inquiries or disputes arising under this agreement shall be subject exclusively to the jurisdiction of the competent courts of Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India.
