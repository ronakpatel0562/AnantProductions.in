Privacy Policy & Client Media Protection

Your wedding moments are sacred family memories. We treat client media, personal data, and delivery vaults with the utmost confidentiality and bank-grade digital security.

Last Updated: October 2026 • Effective for all 2026–2027 Wedding Bookings

1. Our Sacred Commitment to Family Privacy

At Anant Productions, we understand that Indian and Gujarati weddings are not public spectacles; they are deeply personal, emotional, and sacred union milestones celebrated among close family, elders, and loved ones.

This Privacy Policy explains how our Ahmedabad studio collects, uses, stores, and protects personal information, photographs, audio recordings, and cinematic wedding films captured before, during, and after your wedding ceremonies.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information directly essential to planning and executing your wedding coverage:

  • Contact Information: Names of the bride, groom, and point-of-contact family members, phone/WhatsApp numbers, email addresses, and residential/billing addresses.
  • Event Itinerary Details: Wedding dates, muhurat timings, venue addresses in Ahmedabad or across Gujarat, ritual names (Ganesh Sthapan, Mehndi, Sangeet, Mandap Muhurat, Haldi, Jaan Aagman, Jaimala, Kanyadaan, Pheras, Vidaai, Reception), and family preferences.
  • Audio-Visual Media: High-resolution RAW and processed photographs, 4K video clips, family interviews, vows, and ambient audio recorded during the commissioned celebrations.

3. Password-Protected Cloud Gallery Vaults

Every client of Anant Productions is provisioned a private, password-protected online gallery hosted on secure cloud infrastructure with SSL/TLS 256-bit encryption.

  • Only individuals who possess your specific private gallery URL and PIN/password can view or download full-resolution photographs.
  • Couples have full control over sharing links with relatives, NRI family abroad, or guests.
  • Guests without authorization cannot view uncurated master files.

4. Social Media & Portfolio Usage Rights

As an artistic studio, we take immense pride in our work. From time to time, we showcase selected highlights, wedding portraits, or cinema teasers on our official website (anantproductions.in), Instagram (@anantproductions), or YouTube channel.

Complete Privacy Option

We respect that some couples, public figures, or conservative families prefer complete digital anonymity. If you do not wish for any photo or video footage from your wedding to be shared online or in our public portfolio, simply inform us in writing prior to signing the service agreement. We will honor an absolute embargo with zero hesitation.

Zero Commercial Stock Sale: Under no circumstances does Anant Productions ever sell, license, or transfer your wedding imagery or family portraits to third-party commercial image banks, AI-training databases, or external advertisers.

5. Dual-Redundant Backup & Media Retention

Accidental data loss is every couple’s worst nightmare. We safeguard your wedding assets with military-grade redundancy:

  • On-Site Dual Slot Recording: Our cameras capture redundant copies directly to dual high-speed memory cards.
  • Immediate Dual NAS Ingestion: After each shooting day, cards are ingested onto two separate hardware-mirrored Network Attached Storage (NAS) drives at our Ahmedabad studio.
  • Vault Archival: Delivered master retouched images and final 4K films are maintained on our cloud servers for at least 12 months after the final handover, allowing you to re-download if ever needed.

6. Compliance with Indian IT Act & DPDP Act

Anant Productions complies with the Information Technology Act, 2000, and the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act of India. We do not track visitors without consent, sell client email lists, or spam your phone number.
